# Support

For suspected bugs, open a GitHub issue and include:

- Your distribution and version.
- Whether the session is GNOME on Wayland or X11.
- The exact steps to reproduce the issue.
- Relevant output from `~/.local/state/am-auto-clicker/am-auto-clicker.log`, after checking it for private information.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, private files, or unrelated personal information. Compatibility reports should identify the target application/runtime version and describe what was tested.
